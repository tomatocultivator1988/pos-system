1. Getting Started & Login

Bean Brewyage is a web-based point-of-sale system for a café. It runs in any modern browser (Chrome or Edge recommended — Chrome/Edge is required for the receipt printer).

  1. Open the application URL in your browser.
  2. Enter your Username and Password.
  3. Click Sign In. You are taken to your default screen (Dashboard for admins, the Orders screen for kitchen staff).
Note: Sessions require re-login after closing the browser. Logging out requires confirmation (you must enter your PIN again on next login).

Default accounts

Security: Change these default passwords after setup (see Section 11).

2. User Roles & Access

The system has three roles. Each role only sees the screens it is allowed to use.

RoleAccessDescription
AdminEverythingFull control: dashboard, POS, inventory, menu, customers, orders, sales, reports, settings.
CashierPOS · Customers · OrdersRuns sales and can view customers and the kitchen screen. Cannot open dashboard, reports, or settings.
KDS (kitchen)Orders onlySees only the Kitchen Display System. Any other page redirects back to Orders.

The sidebar navigation automatically hides items the current role cannot access.

4. POS Terminal

The POS Terminal is where sales are rung up.

Finding an item

  • Type in the Search items… box to filter the menu.
  • Click a category to show only items in that category.
  • Click an item card to add it.

Variants & add-ons

When an item has options, a dialog opens:

  • Variant — choose the size/type (e.g. 12oz Iced). The price updates automatically.
  • Add-ons — tick any extra items (e.g. extra shot, syrup). Required add-on groups must have at least one selection.
  • Click Add to Cart to confirm.

Cart controls

  • Use + / − to change the quantity of a line item.
  • Use the trash icon to remove a line.
  • Clear Cart empties the whole order.

Customer (optional)

Tap + Select Customer to attach a customer to the sale and earn loyalty points (see Section 10).

Checkout

  1. Click Checkout.
  2. Choose a payment method:
    • Cash — enter the amount tendered. Use the quick buttons (+₱50, +₱100, +₱200, +₱500, +₱1000) or Round Up. Change is calculated automatically.
    • GCash — enter the GCash Reference #. The sale cannot be completed without a reference.
  3. Click Complete Sale, then confirm.
  4. A receipt is shown (and sent to the printer if one is paired).
Note: Complete Sale stays disabled until the amount tendered covers the total (cash) or a reference is entered (GCash).

5. Kitchen Display System (Orders)

The Kitchen Display System (KDS) shows live orders for kitchen staff. It refreshes automatically.

Order states

ColumnStatusAction button
New OrdersnewStart Preparing
Preparing OrderspreparingMark Ready
Ready OrdersreadyComplete

Each order card shows the order number, an elapsed timer since it was placed, the items and any add-ons, and the action button.

  1. Tap Start Preparing when the kitchen begins the order.
  2. Tap Mark Ready when the order is done.
  3. Tap Complete when the customer receives it.

Completed orders move out of the active columns. A yellow banner appears when new orders are waiting.

6. Sales History

The Sales screen lists every transaction with its status and payment details.

ColumnDescription
Order #Unique order number (format ORD-YYYYMMDD-NNNNNN).
Date & TimeWhen the sale was made.
ItemsNumber of line items.
TotalGrand total paid.
PaymentCash or GCash.

Actions

  • View — open the full receipt.
  • Void — cancel the sale (allowed only while it is new or preparing). Requires a reason.
  • Refund — refund the sale (allowed unless it is already refunded). Requires a reason.
Note: Voiding or refunding a sale restores any stock that was deducted from inventory.

8. Inventory

The Inventory screen tracks ingredients and stock levels.

Viewing stock

  • Use the search box to find an ingredient.
  • Filter by All Items, Low Stock, or Out of Stock.
  • The table shows quantity on hand, unit, unit cost, reorder level, and total value.

Adding an ingredient

  1. Click New Ingredient.
  2. Enter the name, unit (g, ml, pcs, etc.), and reorder level.
  3. Click Create.

Adjusting stock

  1. Click Adjust on an ingredient row.
  2. Enter a Delta (+/−) and a Reason.
  3. Click Apply. Stock is updated and a movement is logged.

Movement Log & Daily Usage

Switch tabs between Stock, Movement Log (every change to stock), and Daily Usage (consumption per day).

9. Menu Management

The Menu screen manages products, variants, add-ons, and recipes.

Creating / editing an item

  1. Click New Item (or Edit on an existing card).
  2. Fill in the name, category, price, and loyalty points earned.
  3. Optional: upload an image.
  4. Tick Send to KDS if the item should appear in the kitchen display.
  5. Tick Active to make it available at the POS.
  6. Click Create / Save.

Recipe & Options dialog

Click Recipe on a card to manage:

  • Variants — sizes/types. Each variant can Override the base price or Adjust by a fixed amount.
  • Add-on Groups — groups of extras (required or optional).
  • Recipe — ingredients consumed per item, variant, or add-on (used for stock deduction).

Deactivate / Reactivate

Use the trash icon to deactivate an item (hides it from the POS). Use Show Inactive to see deactivated items and Reactivate them.

10. Customers & Loyalty

The Customers screen tracks customer profiles and loyalty points.

  • Click New Customer, enter the name and optional mobile number / email, then Create.
  • Each customer gets a member number automatically.
  • Use the search box to find a customer.

When a customer is attached to a sale at the POS, they earn loyalty points based on the items purchased. The receipt shows the points earned.

11. Settings & Receipt Printer

The Settings screen (admin only) configures the store and the hardware.

Store Information

Business name, phone, address — shown on receipts.

Business Settings

  • Currency and Timezone (default: PHP, Asia/Manila).
  • Tax rate applied to sales.
  • Low-stock behavior — warn or block sales when an ingredient is out of stock.

Bluetooth Printer & Cash Drawer

Receipts print over Web Bluetooth (Chrome/Edge only). The cash drawer opens through the printer's RJ12 port or a USB drawer.

  • Pair Printer — connect a thermal receipt printer.
  • Test Print — print a test receipt.
  • Test Drawer — open the cash drawer.

Click Save Settings to store all changes.

Note: "Not paired" is shown until a printer has been connected. Only Chrome/Edge support the printer — this is a browser limitation.

12. Receipts & Printing

Every sale produces a receipt showing:

  • Business name, date & time, and order number.
  • Items with quantities, variants, and add-ons.
  • Subtotal, tax, payment method, amount tendered, and change.
  • Loyalty points earned (if a customer was attached).

If a printer is paired, the receipt prints automatically on checkout. You can also re-print from the Sales screen using the receipt view.

13. Troubleshooting

Can't sign in

  • Check the username and password are correct.
  • Make sure the browser session has not expired — refresh and sign in again.

Receipt printer not working

  • Confirm you are using Chrome or Edge (Web Bluetooth is required).
  • Re-pair the printer from Settings → Pair Printer.
  • Run Test Print to verify the connection.

Item won't add to the cart

  • Check the item is Active in Menu Management.
  • Check stock — an out-of-stock item may be blocked (depending on the low-stock setting).

Order missing from the kitchen screen

  • Confirm the item has Send to KDS enabled.
  • Refresh the Orders screen — it polls for updates automatically.
